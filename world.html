BOOK ONE — THE DISTANT SHIP

Every island has its legends. On Varindel, the legend is simple: never sail too far into the Western Sea. For generations, the warning has kept sailors close to shore. Ships that ignore it don't come back — and no one on the island can say why.

A WARNING NO ONE CAN EXPLAIN

For best friends Eric and Jacob, the warning has always been background noise — the kind of story you stop really hearing after a while. The Western Sea is simply part of life on Varindel: beautiful from a distance, but something people learn to respect rather than question.

That changes the day an abandoned lighthouse on the edge of the island — long dismissed as nothing more than a ruin — gives up a secret no one expected to find.

THE LIGHTHOUSE'S SECRET

Inside, Eric and Jacob discover a book no one can read and a symbol no one can explain. It answers nothing. Instead, it becomes the first thread in a mystery that will pull them far from everything they know.

A WIDER WORLD

The search takes them off Varindel entirely, into a sprawling capital city that makes their quiet island look impossibly small. There, they cross paths with Alice Viremont — a presence that ties them to something far bigger than a lighthouse full of old secrets, and makes the world beyond their island feel suddenly, unavoidably personal.

GOING HOME CHANGES EVERYTHING

But it's the trip home that changes everything. Varindel looks the same. It doesn't feel the same. Familiar places carry different meanings now, and moments that once seemed ordinary are impossible to ignore.

THE OTHER VARINDEL

And then, somewhere in the world they thought they knew, Eric and Jacob find something that shouldn't exist: a second Varindel. Every familiar street and face reflected back at them — subtly, horribly wrong.

Why does this place exist? How can something so familiar appear somewhere it shouldn't? The answers are not given in Book One.

WHAT BOOK ONE LEAVES BEHIND

The lighthouse and its strange book remain unexplained. The meaning of the symbol is still unknown. The Western Sea continues to hide more than the people of Varindel understand. Alice is now bound to their journey, and the world has proven far larger — and far stranger — than one small island.

The boys began with curiosity about a distant ship.

What they found was a world full of questions — and it isn't finished with them yet.
